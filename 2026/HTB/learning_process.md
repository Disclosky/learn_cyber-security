"Think outside the box"

Occam's Razor is one of the central principles of modern scientific theory. The principle is based on the following definition:
>The most straightforward theory is preferable to all others of several sufficient possible explanations for the same state of facts. In other words: The simplest explanation is always the most probable.<br>
_Di antara berbagai kemungkinan penjelasan yang sama-sama memadai untuk suatu fakta yang sama, teori yang paling lugas lebih diutamakan dibandingkan yang lain. Dengan kata lain: Penjelasan yang paling sederhana selalu merupakan penjelasan yang paling mungkin benar._

- The simplest explanation for the best approach to penetration testing is that we work with the information we can get.
